About

Built for anyone who needs to time anything.

What is StopwatchPro?

StopwatchPro is a free, browser-based set of timing tools: a stopwatch, lap and split timer, countdown timer, interval timer, alarm clock, digital clock, chess clock, metronome and tally counter. There's nothing to download, no account to create, and no fees. Just open the page and start timing — whether you're on a phone, tablet, or desktop computer.

Why we built it

Most online stopwatches are cluttered, slow to load, or riddled with pop-ups. We built StopwatchPro to be the opposite: fast, clean, and reliable. It works the moment you open it, even on a slow connection.

Who uses StopwatchPro?

  • Athletes and coaches tracking workout splits
  • Students using the Pomodoro technique
  • Cooks timing recipes to the second
  • Teachers managing classroom activities
  • Scientists and researchers recording experiments
  • Anyone who needs a quick, accurate timer

Features

  • Millisecond-accurate timing
  • Nine tools in one site, each with its own guide and FAQ
  • Unlimited lap recording with best/worst split highlights
  • Keyboard shortcuts (Space, L, R)
  • Works on all devices and browsers
  • No ads that interrupt timing
  • 100% free, forever

The tools

  • Stopwatch and lap & split timer for runs, speeches, experiments and any "how long did that take?" question
  • Countdown timer for cooking, study sessions and presentations
  • Interval timer for workouts, circuits and focus-and-break cycles
  • Alarm clock and digital clock for reminders and a large, readable time display
  • Chess clock for two-player timed games
  • Metronome and tally counter for music practice and manual counting

Every tool page explains how the tool works, how to get the most from it, and answers common questions. Start with the how-to guide if you are new.

How it works and your privacy

All of the timing logic runs in your own browser. Saved countdowns, routines and alarms are stored locally on your device, and lap times exist only for your current session. We don't ask you to sign up and we don't collect an account profile. The Privacy Policy explains what cookies and advertising services the site uses.

How the site is funded

StopwatchPro is free to use. To cover hosting and development time we may show advertising on some pages. We keep the tools themselves clean and fast, and we don't place ads over the controls while you are timing.
