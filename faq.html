Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about all of StopwatchPro's timing tools: how they work, what they save, and how to get the most from them.

Is StopwatchPro really free to use?

Yes. StopwatchPro is completely free with no sign-up, no download, and no hidden fees. It runs directly in your browser.

How accurate is the online stopwatch?

StopwatchPro measures time to the millisecond using your browser's high-resolution timing functions, making it accurate enough for workouts, cooking, races, and lab timing.

Does StopwatchPro work without an internet connection?

Yes, once the page has loaded once, the stopwatch continues to function offline since all timing happens locally in your browser.

Can I use StopwatchPro on my phone?

Yes. The layout is responsive and the Start/Lap/Reset controls are large enough for touch use. Keyboard shortcuts apply only on devices with a physical or connected keyboard.

Is there a limit to how many laps I can record?

No — you can record laps for as long as the browser tab stays open and active.

Does StopwatchPro collect or store my timing data?

Timing happens in your browser and is not sent to a server. Lap times are not stored. If you save countdowns, routines or alarms, they are kept in your own browser. See our Privacy Policy for details on cookies and advertising.

Can I run multiple stopwatches at once in different tabs?

Yes, each browser tab runs its own independent instance.

Why does the millisecond display sometimes skip numbers?

Browsers update the display roughly 60 times per second, so at very fast refresh rates you may see the millisecond counter skip by more than 1 between renders — the underlying elapsed-time measurement itself remains accurate.

Do you also have a countdown timer?

Yes — see our Countdown Timer for setting a fixed duration or counting down to a specific time.

Does StopwatchPro save my timers between visits?

Saved countdowns, interval routines and alarms are stored in your browser on your device, so they are there next time you visit on the same browser. They do not sync between devices, and clearing your browser data removes them. Lap times are not saved and clear when you reset or close the tab.

What is the difference between a stopwatch and a countdown timer?

A stopwatch counts up from zero to measure how long something takes. A countdown timer counts down from a set time to zero and alerts you. Use a stopwatch when you do not know the duration in advance, and a countdown when you have a fixed limit.

What is the difference between a countdown timer and an alarm clock?

A countdown timer measures a duration, such as 20 minutes from now. An alarm clock sounds at a time of day, such as 3:15 pm.

What is the difference between a lap and a split?

A lap time is how long one segment took. A split time is the total elapsed time from the start to that point. The lap list shows both.

Will the alarm go off if I close the tab?

No. The alarm runs in the browser tab, so the tab must stay open and your device awake. It can be in the background, but do not rely on it for anything critical.

Why does my timer seem slow or late in a background tab?

Browsers and phones can slow background tabs to save battery. Keep the tab visible and your screen awake for important timing.

Can I hear the alerts on my phone?

Yes, if your device volume is up and sound is not blocked. Some browsers only allow sound after you have interacted with the page, so press a button once or use Preview before relying on an alert.

How do I repeat rounds in the interval timer?

The interval timer runs your list of steps once from top to bottom. To repeat a pattern, add the steps again for each round, then save the routine. Our Tabata and HIIT guide shows an example.

Does the chess clock support increments?

No. It gives both players the same fixed number of minutes with no added time per move.

What BPM range does the metronome cover?

The slider covers 30 to 240 beats per minute, and a tap-tempo button sets the tempo from your taps.

Which browsers does it work in?

Any modern browser on desktop or mobile, including recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.

Can I change the colours or make the text larger?

Yes. Use the palette button for colours, large text and high-contrast modes, and the moon button for dark mode. Your choices are remembered in your browser.

Which languages are supported?

The interface can be switched between English, Spanish, French, German and Dutch. The long written guides are in English.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

Use the contact page. Please say which tool you were using, which browser and device you have, and what happened.

Does StopwatchPro show ads or use cookies?

The site may use cookies and advertising services. The Privacy Policy explains what is used and how to control it.

StopwatchPro is created and maintained by Kent Williams in Houston, Texas. See the About page for more.
